EmailJS & Mail Settings

EverLocker uses EmailJS to send automated email notifications directly to learners when you reply to their queries.

Template Variables Guide

Ensure your EmailJS email template uses these variables:

  • {{to_name}} — Learner name
  • {{to_email}} — Learner email address
  • {{reply_message}} — Admin reply content
  • {{from_name}} — EverLocker Support
